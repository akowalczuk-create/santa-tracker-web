# Elf Express Playtest

## What this is
Elf Express is a new Santa Tracker game. There are 10 versions, A to J. They're the same game with different difficulty, and we want to find the version that's easy to start, gets harder in a way that feels fair, and makes you want to play again.

## Known issue
The snowball doesn't fully work yet—you can still dodge it, but getting hit may not slow you down. Please ignore it and rate how fast and busy the game feels; versions can still be compared fairly.

## How to test
- Play the versions in this order: **A, F, C, H, E, J, B, I, D, G**. Play each at least twice if you can.
- Use a phone if you can.
- Fill in a row in the rating sheet right after each game.

## Rating sheet guide
Open the **"Elf Express balance test ratings"** sheet, **"Plays"** tab:

- **Version**: the letter in the link.
- **Date**: the day you played.
- **Tester**: your name (or "Name + kid, age 8" if a child played).
- **Device**: Phone (touch), Tablet (touch), or Computer (mouse).
- **Sleigh 1 needed**: how many gifts the first sleigh asked for. This checks you're on the right version.
- **Furthest sleigh reached**: the last sleigh you got to, 1 to 8.
- **Beat all 8?**: Yes or No.
- **Sleighs 1-3 felt / Sleighs 4-6 felt / Sleighs 7-8 felt**: Too easy, Just right, or Too hard. Leave blank if you didn't get that far.
- **Fun (1-5)**: 1 = boring or frustrating, 5 = loved it.
- **Play again?**: Yes, Maybe, or No.
- **Notes**: anything you noticed, especially where you got bored, where it got overwhelming, and what made you lose.
